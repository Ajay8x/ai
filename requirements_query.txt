# JARVIS AI - SUPPORTED VOICE COMMANDS
# Aap Jarvis se ye sabhi sawal/commands pooch sakte hain:

--- 🌐 INTERNET & SEARCH ---
- "Search what is Python" (Wikipedia/Google par search karega)
- "Google Elon Musk" 
- "Play Arijit Singh" (YouTube par gaana/video chalayega)
- "Weather" ya "Weather in Mumbai" (Mausam batayega)
- "News" (Top 3 taaza khabrein batayega)

--- 💻 SYSTEM & APPS ---
- "Open Chrome" (Google Chrome khulega)
- "Open YouTube" (Website khulegi)
- "Open Settings" (PC ki settings khulegi)
- "Close Chrome" (App band karega)
- "Close Tab" ya "Close window" (Samne khula tab band karega)
- "Take a screenshot" (Screen ki photo lega)

--- 🔊 MEDIA & VOLUME ---
- "Volume up" (Awaaz badhayega)
- "Volume down" (Awaaz kam karega)
- "Mute" (Awaaz band karega)
- "Pause" (Chal raha gaana/video rokega)
- "Resume" (Ruka hua gaana/video wapas chalayega)

--- 🔋 PC CONTROL & INFO ---
- "Shutdown" (PC band karega)
- "Restart" (PC restart karega)
- "Sleep" (PC ko sleep mode me dalega)
- "Check Battery" (Battery kitni hai aur charge ho rahi hai ya nahi, batayega)
- "Check CPU" (CPU par kitna load hai, batayega)
- "Time" (Abhi ka time batayega)
- "Date" (Aaj ki date batayega)

--- ⏰ ALARMS & TIMERS ---
- "Set a timer for 10 minutes" (10 minute ka timer lagayega)
- "Set an alarm for 7 AM" (Subah 7 baje ka alarm lagayega)

--- 🧠 MEMORY ---
- "Remember that my keys are on the table" (Aapki baat yaad rakhega)
- "What do you remember?" (Jo yaad rakhne ko kaha tha, wo batayega)

--- ❌ EXIT ---
- "Stop" ya "Exit" (Jarvis ko band kar dega)
